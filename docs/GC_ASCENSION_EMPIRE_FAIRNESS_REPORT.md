## 5. Verdict for the reference candidate

Thresholds (this analysis' operationalisation, shared with the S1 study): cumulative production within 0.85-1.15 of `never` at 48 months (median) and no style above 1.25 at 48 / 72 months; payback within 365 days (good) or 730 days (ok) for every style; extra resets do not pay (no style gains more than 0.05 from 4 resets over a single one).

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Scale: the whole empire (`O200`) stays within the window | yes |
| Scope: no scope (one mine / serial / parallel) pushes a style above 1.25 | yes |
| Payback of the whole empire, judged on the slowest style | no (median / range: 641 (633-802) days) |
| Guard: farming and the ladder do not pay | yes |
| **All** | **no** |

Parallel vs serial: cumulative production at 48 months differs by 0.190 (`O200` minus `G200`).

## 6. Limits

* One homeworld seed (eligible DNA), one reward family, fixed policies; no Research-Lab Ascension, Stellar Forge, loot or Timekeeper.  Several DNA seeds belong to the PL10 robustness step.
* The baseline includes the outpost-XP defect (#556 not applied); it affects colony founding, not the mine rules priced here.
* `G200` is serial in the sense of the policy: a mine that is ready waits for the empire-wide rebuild slot and keeps building meanwhile, so it resets deeper than L200.
* Thresholds are this analysis' operationalisation.
