## 6. Limits

* The world seed is not a suite: it pins only the homeworld's coordinates and DNA, and the simulated economy is identical for every seed (tested).  Per-planet world-type multipliers, trait effects and specialisations are not modelled, so planet-to-planet variation of the mine economy is outside this model.
* Policies are fixed; a smarter or a clumsier player than the policies here may exist.  Thresholds are this analysis' operationalisation.
* The baseline includes the outpost-XP defect (#556 not applied); one homeworld seed (eligible DNA) in S11.
